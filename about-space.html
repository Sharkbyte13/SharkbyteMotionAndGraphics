The Other Side of the Universe

The Other Side
of the Universe

Most designers don't spend their evenings analysing stellar photometry or modelling quantum systems. Here's why that changes everything about how I design.

✦

Design was never the detour. It was the other current running underneath. Both disciplines ask the same fundamental question: how do you take something complex and render it clear?

✦

Physics trains you to distrust shortcuts. When you've spent time verifying that a signal is real and not an artifact of your reduction pipeline, you develop a healthy skepticism of the first answer — in science and in design.
